About

Hi, I'm Pedro.

I'm a university student from Portugal, currently based in Madrid, and I build websites for small businesses.

I started Oakplane because I think most small business websites all look the same, and a business worth remembering should have a site that feels like it. I like building things that are a little different, and I like making them work properly too.

When you work with me, you talk to me directly. I explain things in plain language, I tell you the price before we start, and I test every site myself before you see it.

why-niche.txt

Why niche?

Because a site that looks like everyone else's is easy to forget.

This site is the proof of what I can do: it is built like a tiny computer, with its own icons, its own navigation and its own personality. Your site does not need to be retro. It needs to feel like your business. A few examples of where I would start:

  • A board-game café with a site that feels like opening a game box.
  • A tattoo studio with a gallery that looks like a flash sheet.
  • A local tour guide with a page that reads like a travel map.
