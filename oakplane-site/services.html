faq.txt

Quick answers.

How long does it take?

About two weeks from the first call to launch, as long as I have your logo, text and photos in time.

What do I need to send you?

Your logo, brand colours, the text for each page and real photos if you have them. I can help with stock photos from Unsplash.

Who owns the website?

You do. Your domain stays in your own account, and at launch I hand over your domain and hosting access.

How does payment work?

50% as a deposit before I start and 50% when the site goes live. Work begins once the deposit is in.

What if I want changes?

Two rounds of revisions are included. I ask you to send all feedback in one message so each round is quick.
